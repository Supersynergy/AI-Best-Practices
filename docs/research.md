# Research methods and limits

The guide combines previously developed workflow principles, a current review of primary sources and executed implementation evidence. Private source notes and raw task history stay outside the public repository. The public text is a synthesis in ordinary engineering language, with no dependency on a private prompt or skill collection.

- Scoped search results included irrelevant names and repositories. Source ownership and exact documents were verified before use. Some official-page fetches failed; alternate primary-source access was used where available. A failed fetch was not evidence of a general Internet outage.
- Current development guidance was checked against DORA, Microsoft Research, METR and official build/test documentation. The [source ledger](sources.md) states each reference's scope. Some publisher pages were inaccessible and are not used as the basis for quantitative claims.
- The twenty principles are editorial guidance, not a validated ranking or a benchmark of the entire workflow. Frequency of recorded local use does not establish effectiveness. Inapplicable numeric targets and private orchestration assumptions were excluded.
- Independent model review of the resource runner remains unavailable because a runtime delegation guard could not verify the requested invocation. The guard was preserved; self/static inspection remains separate from independent review.
- Short process and device write samples give partial attribution on different measurement layers. They do not establish complete write paths, NAND endurance or daily rates.

The software is experimental. Local tests and static review do not substitute for an independent review. No SSD-life extension, paid provider savings or universal hardware optimum is claimed. Published synthetic measurements remain separate from current host status and whole-workflow productivity.
